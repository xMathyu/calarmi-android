Calarmi comes to Android:
• Alarms that ring even when your phone is silent, full screen over the lock screen.
• Flexible repeats, several schedules and up to 5 alerts per alarm.
• Share alarms and countdowns with anyone, on iPhone or Android.
• Wake-up challenges, alarms for calendar events, countdowns with widgets and an on-device assistant.
• Optional Calarmi Plus: Arcade challenges, your own tones, unlimited countdowns and colored icons.
